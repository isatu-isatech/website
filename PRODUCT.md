# Product: ISATech Society Website

## What this is

The public website for **ISATech Society** (full name: _ISAT U Innovators and Technopreneurs Society_), a student-led organization at Iloilo Science and Technology University (ISAT U) in Iloilo, Western Visayas, Philippines. Deployed at **https://isatech.club**.

It is the org's primary public presence: a marketing/perception surface that introduces who they are, showcases their work and achievements, recruits members, and funnels visitors toward membership, events, and contact.

## Mission & positioning

- Mission (as stated in site copy): **"Empowering student founders to achieve their dreams through innovation, collaboration, and community."**
- Site taglines: _"Empowering the Next Generation of Innovators"_, _"Empowering student founders to achieve their dreams."_
- Motto scroll: **DREAM · INNOVATE · SUCCEED**
- Founded: 2021 (founding year recorded as 2021).
- The org runs a **"4H" roles** framework — the four distinct roles/types members can embody (Hustler, Hypeman, Hacker, Hipster — e.g. "The designer", "The researcher", etc.), which is also the basis of an interactive on-site quiz.

## Primary users / audiences

The site serves **four overlapping audiences** (all confirmed as primary):

1. **Prospective / new members** (ISAT U students) — deciding whether to join; need to understand what the org does and how to sign up.
2. **Event attendees & competition participants** — already engaged with org programs (e.g. Codelympics), seeking info, recaps, and updates.
3. **Current members & officers** — the org's insiders; a hub for identity, news, and membership info.
4. **The public / sponsors / industry** — evaluating the org's credibility, partners, record, and reach.

## What the website is for (success looks like)

All four goals are active and shared:

- **Recruit & convert new members** — drive student signups via the membership and contact flows.
- **Build credibility & visibility** — showcase achievements, events, the team, officers/advisers, and partners.
- **Engage visitors** — the interactive "Which 4H role are you?" quiz and rich content keep visitors engaged and drive awareness.
- **Capture contact / partnership / sponsor inquiries** — lead-gen through the contact form (Cloudflare Turnstile protected) and channels.

## Site structure (surfaces)

- **Home** — marketing landing: YouTube-video hero, stats, about, partners (incl. KWADRA TBI), team, "what we offer", contact.
- **About** — mission/vision, the org, advisers carousel, decorations.
- **Membership** — how to join and member benefits.
- **Quiz** — interactive "Which 4H role are you?" quiz with intro/question/result screens.
- **Contact** — turnstile-protected form + contact info.
- **Privacy** — privacy policy.

## Platform

`web` — a responsive website (desktop-first marketing/landing experience, mobile-aware). No native app.

## Accessibility

Site already targets core a11y (Radix UI primitives, semantic HTML). Accessibility is a standing requirement — any redesign/rebuild must preserve and improve it.

## Scope decision (active direction)

The confirmed direction for upcoming design work is a **redesign / rebrand of the visual world**. The incumbent look (blue/gold, current layout) is treated as **evidence and anti-reference**; the product's truth, content, functions, native affordances, and technical constraints are preserved, but the visual language will be replaced. New DESIGN.md work proceeds via the new-work flow.
